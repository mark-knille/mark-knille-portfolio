Podcast Episode Production System

How I turned a scramble of one-off assets into a repeatable package, so every episode of The Footwear Retailer ships complete, on brand, and ready for the client and sponsor.

ClientThe Footwear Retailer podcast, hosted by Pete Mohr and sponsored by NSRA
My roleWorkflow design, creative direction, design and video production, QA
ScopeEvery episode: SEO, client reporting, scheduling, sponsor kit, social graphics, video
ToolsBrand-kit templates, AI-assisted workflows, Riverside, Descript, Canva

The challenge

A podcast episode is more than a video. Each one needs search-ready titles and descriptions, a deck for the client, a posting plan, a separate package for the sponsor, and a set of social graphics, all in the show’s brand. Building that from scratch every episode is slow and invites inconsistency.

The system

I standardized the whole package. The show’s brand kit (colors, fonts, logo) feeds a set of templates, and an AI-assisted workflow drafts copy from the episode transcript. I review, refine, and QA every piece before it goes out.

Result

Each episode now ships as one consistent package instead of a pile of one-off requests. The client gets a predictable set of deliverables, the sponsor gets its own posting kit, and new episodes start from a proven template instead of a blank page.
