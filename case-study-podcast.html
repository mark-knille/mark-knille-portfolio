The challenge

A podcast episode is more than a video. Each one needs an edit, short clips for social, launch graphics for every channel, copy for every post, and a separate plan for the sponsor, all in the show’s brand. Building that from scratch every episode is slow and invites inconsistency.

The system

I standardized the whole package. The show’s brand kit (colors, fonts, logo) feeds a set of templates, and AI-assisted tools prepare the edit and draft copy from the transcript. I review, refine, and QA every piece before it goes out.

Episode edit

The full 46-minute episode. An AI-assisted first pass flagged 46 edit points before the human polish.

Social reels

5 vertical reels, picked from 23 clip candidates the edit pass surfaced.

Launch graphics

One on-brand asset per posting step: teaser, email header, quote posts, story, carousel, and thumbnail.

Sponsor posting pack

A 7-page playbook for NSRA with the artwork, timing, channel, and ready-to-paste copy for every post.

Copy

Captions, hashtags, and two email subject lines to A/B test, all written from the transcript.

Quality check

Every quote is checked against what the guest actually said before anything is released.

The sponsor rollout

The Episode 28 posting pack for NSRA: eight posts across five channels over a week, each with its own artwork, timing, and copy. Click any artwork to view it full size.
