Client Project Workspaces in Notion & Trello

How I keep several client projects moving at once, with one shared place for every request, deadline, round of feedback, and approval.

ClientsMultiple concurrent client accounts
My roleDesigned, set up, and ran the client workspaces
ScopeIntake, scheduling, status tracking, feedback, approvals, handoff
ToolsNotion, Trello

The challenge

With several clients at once, requests come in through email, chat, and calls, and feedback lands in different threads. Without one source of truth, deadlines slip and nobody is sure which version was approved.

The workflow

Every request moves through the same stages, so clients and the team always know what happens next.

  1. 1Intake

    Requests captured with goals, specs, and due dates

  2. 2Brief

    Scope, owner, and timeline agreed

  3. 3In production

    Tasks tracked with status and priority

  4. 4Client review

    Feedback rounds logged on the card

  5. 5Approved

    Sign-off recorded before final export

  6. 6Delivered

    Final files handed off and archived

Inside the workspaces

Screenshots from client workspaces. Client names and sensitive details are hidden for confidentiality. Click any image to view it full size.

Result

Clients can see where their project stands at a glance, feedback stays attached to the work it’s about, and every approval is on record. That makes it possible to run several accounts in parallel without things slipping between revision rounds.
